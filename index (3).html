About the academy

Preserving heritage, inspiring expression

At Shiv Shakti Dance Academy, we aim to preserve and promote India's rich dance heritage while inspiring creativity, discipline and self-expression in every student.

Our mission is to build confidence and bring out the best in every dancer — through the divine art of movement.

  • Structured curriculum from beginner to advanced
  • Regular stage performances and recitals
  • Classes for kids, teens and adults alike
